From open role to paid reward in three steps

  1. Post roles and set the reward

    Add the jobs you're hiring for and decide what a successful referral is worth for each one.

  2. Your team refers people they trust

    Employees submit a candidate directly or share a personal link. Either way the credit lands with them.

  3. Move candidates along and pay out

    Update each referral as it progresses. When someone is hired, the reward is queued for you to mark as paid.

Built for both sides of a referral

People stop referring when their candidate disappears into a black hole. Referleey keeps the hiring team organised and the referrer informed.

For the hiring team

  • One pipeline for every referralSearch, filter by stage, and open any referral to see its full history.
  • Rewards that track themselvesMarking someone as hired queues the right reward. You see what's owed and what's paid.
  • A careers page out of the boxEvery company gets a public page listing its open roles.
  • Export when you need itDownload the pipeline as a spreadsheet for payroll or reporting.

For employees

  • Refer in under a minuteName, email and a line about why they'd be good. That's the whole form.
  • A personal link for every jobShare it anywhere. Anyone who applies through it is credited to you.
  • Live status on every candidateSee exactly where each person stands without chasing a recruiter.
  • A leaderboard worth climbingPoints for every referral and every hire, visible to the whole company.

Fair by default

The rules that cause arguments in referral programs are settled by the system, the same way for everyone.

First referral keeps the credit

A candidate can only be put forward once per job, so there's never a dispute over who referred whom.

No self-referrals

Employees can't refer themselves, by form or by link.

Your company's data stays yours

Each company has a separate workspace. Nobody outside it can see your roles, people or candidates.

Paid rewards are locked

Once a reward is marked as paid, the referral's outcome can't be quietly changed.
